What we offer
Our core 3D services
A full-spectrum studio for every stage of your 3D journey — from initial model to final delivery.

3D Modeling

Precision-crafted models built to exact specifications. From concept sketches to production-ready geometry, every mesh is optimized for its intended use.

  • High-poly and low-poly modeling
  • Organic and hard-surface modeling
  • Retopology and mesh optimization
  • CAD-to-3D conversion
  • File formats: OBJ, FBX, STL, GLTF

3D Animation

Compelling motion that brings products, characters, and environments to life. We craft frame-perfect animation sequences for broadcast, digital, and interactive use.

  • Product and explainer animations
  • Character rigging and keyframe animation
  • Mechanical and process simulations
  • Motion graphics integration
  • Loop-ready assets for web and app

3D Rendering & Visualization

Photorealistic imagery that makes stakeholders feel, not just see. Ideal for pre-launch marketing, investor decks, and design approvals.

  • Architectural walkthroughs and stills
  • Product lifestyle renders
  • 360° panoramic views
  • Day/night and material variants
  • Print-ready and web-ready outputs

AR / VR Experiences

Interactive immersive environments built for enterprise training, retail, real estate, and branded activations. Deploy across headsets, mobile, and web.

  • WebAR and mobile AR (iOS/Android)
  • VR training and simulation environments
  • Virtual showrooms and product configurators
  • Oculus, HoloLens, and WebXR support
  • Real-time interaction design

Product Design & Visualization

A seamless bridge between industrial design and market-ready presentation. We help product teams validate aesthetics, communicate design intent, and accelerate go-to-market.

  • Concept ideation and 3D sketching
  • Variant and colorway explorations
  • Packaging and label visualization
  • Pre-production prototype renders
  • Design-for-manufacturing review

Common questions
Frequently asked questions

What files do I need to provide to get started?

We can work from sketches, reference photos, CAD files, or a brief description. The more detail you provide, the faster we can move. We'll guide you through exactly what's needed during the discovery call.

How long does a typical 3D project take?

Timelines vary by scope. A single product render takes 3–5 business days; a full AR/VR experience may take 4–8 weeks. You'll receive a clear timeline in your project proposal before work begins.

Do you offer rush delivery?

Yes. Expedited timelines are available for most service types, subject to availability. Rush fees apply and will be noted transparently in your quote.

Will I own the source files?

Full IP ownership and source file transfer is included by default upon project completion. We retain no commercial rights to your deliverables.

Can you integrate with our existing design or dev team?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with in-house design teams, developers, and agencies. We're comfortable working within existing pipelines, version control systems, and project management tools.

Ready to bring your vision to life?

Tell us about your project — our team will respond within one business day with a tailored proposal.
